# Kandang Manajemen — Modern Glassmorphism & Warm Paper System
> Modern glassmorphic warmth tailored for poultry broiler farm management on mobile

## Visual Identity & Tokens
- **Canvas Base**: `#f6f5f4` (Warm off-white tactile canvas) with subtle atmospheric fluid gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, #f0ede8 0%, #e8f0fe 50%, #fef5ea 100%)`)
- **Glassmorphic Cards**: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); box-shadow: 0 8px 32px 0 rgba(31, 38, 135, 0.05); border-radius: 16px;`
- **Primary Color**: `#0075de` (Notion Blue for primary actions & active states)
- **Status & Accent Colors**:
  - Sky Tint: `#e6f3fe`
  - Marigold / Amber: `#ffb110` (Perhatian, saran berikutnya)
  - Coral: `#f64932` (Penting/peringatan)
  - Ink Black: `#000000` (80% / 60% / 40% alpha)
  - Stone Gray: `#757575`
- **Typography**: Inter (modern sans-serif, legible at small mobile viewports, bold numbers, clear rupiah formatting)
- **Layout & Spacing**: 4px base grid, card padding 16px-20px, rounded corners 16px for glass containers, 10px-12px for buttons and badges, 9999px for pills.
- **Tone & Copy**: Bahasa Indonesia lugas, format Rupiah bulat penuh (contoh: `Rp150.000`), status eksplisit sesuai brief, tanpa istilah asing teknis.
